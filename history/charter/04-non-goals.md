# 04 - Non-Goals

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Project | AegisBank AI Platform |
| Milestone | `v0.1.0 - Project Charter` |
| Status | Ready for review |

## Purpose

This document is an enforceable anti-scope-creep boundary for `v1.0`. A feature
being technically possible, fashionable, present in a job description, or useful
for a certification is not sufficient reason to add it.

The project demonstrates enterprise engineering through a narrow fictitious
workflow. It does not attempt to reproduce a bank.

## Explicit Non-Goals For v1.0

### Real institutions and production use

- Integration with Mibanco or any real bank, lender, payment processor, credit bureau, regulator, or enterprise system.
- Presentation as official software of Mibanco, Microsoft, the SBS, PCM, NIST, or another institution.
- Production use, pilot use with real customers, or shadow operation beside a real banking workflow.
- A legal, regulatory, security, privacy, NIST, or banking compliance certification or attestation.
- Service-level commitments or operational claims for a regulated financial institution.

### Real data and identity

- Real customers, employees, approvers, or operators represented in datasets or fixtures.
- Real DNI, CE, passport, tax, phone, email, address, biometric, demographic, account, loan, card, credential, or transaction data.
- Scraped, leaked, purchased, anonymized, or pseudonymized real banking data.
- Real bank authentication, production Entra tenants, or consumer identity federation in early phases.
- Secrets, tokens, passwords, certificates, or connection strings committed to Git.

Synthetic data must not be generated by copying or lightly perturbing an identifiable real record.

### Financial operations and consequential decisions

- Real or simulated money movement, including transfers, payments, withdrawals, deposits, collections, refunds, and disbursements.
- Card issuing, card authorization, card tokenization, wallets, or payment rails.
- Automatic credit approval, denial, pricing, limit setting, restructuring, collections prioritization, or production credit scoring.
- A production fraud decision or suspicious-activity adjudication system.
- Financial, legal, investment, or credit advice.
- Biometric enrollment, authentication, recognition, emotion inference, or liveness detection.

The only `v1.0` write is creation of a fictitious service request or complaint.

### Product and channel breadth

- A complete core banking platform or high-fidelity clone of an existing bank.
- A complete customer-service suite, CRM, case-management platform, loan origination system, or collections system.
- A native mobile application.
- A real WhatsApp, SMS, email, voice, call-center, or social-media integration in the initial release.
- Omnichannel conversation synchronization.
- Broad back-office RPA or desktop automation.
- Dozens of product types, customer journeys, or state-changing actions.

### Premature architecture

- Microservices from the first milestone or extraction without lifecycle, scale, ownership, security-boundary, or failure-isolation evidence.
- Multiple agents before the single agent has evaluations, controlled tools, HITL, audit, tracing, reliability, and tests.
- Multi-agent orchestration as a `v1.0` requirement.
- AKS before local execution, Docker, CI, Container Apps, security, and observability are proven.
- Kafka or another event-streaming platform when Azure Service Bus satisfies the defined messaging need.
- Event sourcing, CQRS, service mesh, distributed workflow engine, or a custom scheduler without demonstrated need.
- Blockchain or an immutable ledger for audit.
- Redis or another cache before a measured latency, contention, or coordination requirement exists.
- Fourteen databases, polyglot persistence, or a separate vector database merely to list technologies.

### Unbounded AI and ML work

- Training a foundation model or proprietary LLM.
- Fine-tuning a large LLM merely to include fine-tuning in the portfolio.
- A neural network without a documented hypothesis, baseline, dataset, metric, and conclusion.
- Autonomous writes, self-modifying prompts, self-granted tools, or LLM-based authorization.
- General web browsing or arbitrary source ingestion as an authoritative RAG corpus.
- Unlimited conversational memory or retention of full conversations by default.
- Claiming that a handful of manual prompts constitutes an evaluation.

### Multi-cloud and infrastructure breadth

- AWS and Azure implementations in parallel.
- A second cloud solely for comparison.
- Paid Azure resources without explicit cost and deployment authorization.
- `v1.0` dependence on AKS; AKS remains an optional `v1.1` experiment.
- Infrastructure work before the corresponding local capability and test gates exist.
- Global modifications to Windows, Git, Python, Docker, Azure CLI, PowerShell, IDEs, or OpenCode as a project shortcut.

## Deferred, Not Promised

The following ideas may be evaluated only after `v1.0`; listing them does not
create a commitment:

| Candidate | Earliest point | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AKS deployment experiment | `v1.1` | A scaling, orchestration, isolation, or learning hypothesis not met by Container Apps |
| Multi-agent experiment | `v1.2` | A measured single-agent limitation and an evaluation plan proving benefit over added risk |
| Additional write action | After `v1.0` | New risk analysis, HITL policy, idempotency design, contracts, and explicit scope approval |
| Real channel adapter | After `v1.0` | Security/privacy review, sandbox environment, cost approval, and no real customer data |
| Small Transformer | Research phase | Baseline, hypothesis, versioned dataset, resource budget, and comparison protocol |

## Prohibited Claims

Project documentation, demos, CV text, and portfolio material must not state or imply:

- that AegisBank is a real or licensed bank;
- that it is endorsed by Mibanco, Microsoft, the SBS, PCM, NIST, or another institution;
- that synthetic-data-only design certifies compliance with Peru's data-protection law;
- that reference to NIST AI RMF constitutes NIST certification;
- that a fictitious high-risk control set has been legally validated for production banking;
- that planned roadmap functionality already exists;
- that a test or evaluation passed when it was not executed.

Permitted wording includes "educational prototype," "fictitious banking
laboratory," "designed with reference to," and "evaluated against the project's
documented criteria."

## Scope-Creep Test

Before accepting any technology or feature, answer all three questions:

1. What approved requirement does it resolve?
2. What concrete risk does it reduce?
3. What defined competency does it teach or prove?

At least one answer must be specific and supported, and the added complexity must
be proportionate. Generic answers such as "enterprise," "scalable," "modern," or
"requested by the market" do not qualify.

### Examples

| Proposal | Decision under current charter | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add idempotency to complaint creation | Accept in `v0.7` | Prevents duplicate business effects and makes safe retries testable |
| Add a second agent to summarize tool output | Reject before `v1.0` | A deterministic function or the existing agent can perform the bounded task |
| Add Kafka for one event | Reject | No defined throughput or ecosystem requirement justifies it |
| Add Azure Service Bus when asynchronous complaint events are implemented | Evaluate in `v0.7`/`v0.9` | Matches a concrete decoupling and AI-200 learning objective |
| Add transfer simulation | Reject for `v1.0` | Expands financial risk without adding a necessary control lesson |
| Add AKS in week one | Reject | Violates local-first roadmap and hides application maturity problems |

## Exception Process

A non-goal can change only through explicit user approval and a documented change
that updates the relevant charter sections before implementation. The change must
include impact on security, privacy, cost, operations, tests, evaluations,
schedule, threat model, and Definition of Done. An ADR records architecture
choices but does not silently override this product boundary.
